# Greater Art — Offline Android Media Player

Greater Art is a native Kotlin/Jetpack Compose player for local music and video.
Scans supported media under Android Download folder. No ads, analytics, accounts,
telemetry, or network access.

Features: recursive scan, 300-item staged thumbnail warmup, Media3 playback,
synchronized .lrc lyrics + embedded fallback, Off→One→All→Random cycle,
three floating modes (Mini default), four backgrounds (liquid metal default),
bluetooth disconnect pauses, FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON.

Privacy: no INTERNET permission. MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE for Download scan only.
Background videos mute audio, pause off-screen, release on disposal.

Build: JDK 21 + Android SDK API 37. See HANDOFF.md.
v1.7.2 quarantined — known crash, not a baseline.

# LocalKit — Your files. Your device. Your tools.

25 focused tools: Scan, Documents, Media, Files, Calculate, Pocket tools.

QR/OCR models bundled (increases APK size, no first-use downloads).
